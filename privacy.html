What FOUND THIS accesses

After the owner grants permission, FOUND THIS can upload videos, read the status and statistics of those videos, and retrieve the owner's non-monetary YouTube Analytics reports.

How data is used

Data is used only to publish owner-approved original content, confirm publication and calculate local performance indicators that guide future editorial experiments. It is not sold, used for advertising profiles or shared with unrelated third parties.

Storage and retention

OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted for the current Windows user with Windows DPAPI. Raw YouTube data and derived local metrics are deleted after no more than 30 days under the current pre-approval policy. Authorisation is checked at least every 30 days.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke Google access and remove local YouTube-authorised data through the FOUND THIS revocation command. The owner can also remove local YouTube data without revoking access. These operations do not delete videos already hosted on YouTube.

Google controls

The owner may also manage third-party access from their Google Account connections. See the Google Privacy Policy.
